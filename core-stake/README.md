# CoreStake

## Project Description and Purpose

CoreStake revolutionizes blockchain gaming by combining skill-based mining simulation with geological authenticity and economic sustainability. Players extract digital resources from procedurally generated core layers of virtual planets using NFT mining equipment with realistic degradation mechanics. This innovative approach creates a balanced ecosystem where strategic resource allocation between equipment maintenance, upgrades, and governance participation drives meaningful player engagement beyond traditional pay-to-win models.

Built on the Stacks blockchain, CoreStake employs a sophisticated dual-layer architecture that leverages Bitcoin's security while enabling real-time gameplay. Layer 1 manages ownership and high-value transactions, while Layer 2 handles dynamic gameplay through state channels with periodic settlement. The game's deflationary token model, combined with skill-based progression and collaborative guild expeditions, creates multiple pathways to success while maintaining long-term economic health through transparent probability mechanics and sustainable revenue streams.

## Key Features

• **Skill-Based Mining Simulation**: Dynamic difficulty adjustment based on network hashrate simulation ensures consistent reward distribution, rewarding player efficiency and strategic thinking rather than pure investment

• **NFT Equipment System**: Mining tools with realistic degradation mechanics require ongoing maintenance and strategic upgrades, creating natural deflationary pressure while preventing equipment oversaturation

• **Dual-Layer Architecture**: Layer 1 Stacks contracts handle ownership and valuable assets while Layer 2 state channels manage real-time gameplay with periodic settlement to Bitcoin for ultimate security

• **Community Governance Council**: Players stake CORE tokens and NFT equipment to vote on planet generation parameters, resource scarcity rates, and equipment balance updates, ensuring community-driven development

• **Guild-Based Deep Core Expeditions**: Collaborative gameplay mechanics reward teamwork in challenging deep mining operations, fostering community engagement and shared success

• **Geological Education Integration**: Real-world mineral distribution data influences game mechanics through university partnerships, providing educational value while enhancing authenticity

• **Transparent Probability Oracles**: Equipment discovery odds based on actual geological surveys replace traditional loot boxes with verifiable, skill-influenced probability systems

• **Multiple Revenue Streams**: Sustainable economics through equipment crafting fees, planet exploration licenses, and peer-to-peer trading percentages, reducing dependence on initial NFT sales while maintaining long-term viability
